Software

Several codes have been developed to capture within short computation time different aspects of additive manufacturing.

Thermal analysis

GreenMelt

Part-Scale Ultra-Fast Melt Pool Prediction in Additive Manufacturing for Latent Heat and Marangoni Convection

Features

  • Green’s function thermal model for fast melt pool evaluation in LPBF and laser surface scanning
  • Accounts for nonlinearities by adding distributed heat sources
    • Latent heat (solidification and vaporization)
    • Marangoni convection
  • Physically based procedures to determine the distribution of additional heat sources to capture nonlinearities
  • Neumann boundary conditions available
  • Validated on single track numerical experiments against Computation Fluid Dynamic
  • Standalone melt pool calculation is available provided material parameters and an average temperature upstream the heat source (i.e., in the solid ahead the melt pool)
  • For part-scale calculation, currently a single back-and-forth scanning strategy is available for cuboid domains

Microstructure

TesselAM

TesselAM is a very fast algorithm for the computation of the formation of microstructure in rapid solidification and further evolution

Features

  • Operates at grain scale, not dendritic scale
  • Physically based geometrical procedure to capture competitive growth in solidification
  • Based on Voronoi tessellations
  • Dramatic reduction of degrees of freedom with respect to cellular automata and phase field approaches
  • Microstructures similar to those from conventional approaches are obtained

Mechanics

QuadWire

QuadWire is a one-dimensional extended mechanical model designed for additive manufacturing

Features

  • Solid parts are obtained by accumulation of elongated beads.
  • Multi-particular approach containing 4 particles per material point
  • Capable of capturing complex 3D mechanical states
  • Significantly reducing the number of degress of freedom with respect to conventional approaches.
